In March 2024, the WWTF opened its first call for research on ME/CFS: small exploratory projects, up to €100,000 each, to find out which approaches hold up.

The plan had two stages from the start. First, try many ideas on a small scale. Then fund the most promising ones on a large scale.

Six months, one call

  1. 03/2024Call opensfor researchers at a Vienna institution, international partners welcome
  2. 06/202419 proposalsfrom 11 institutions, 3 of them joint projects
  3. 09/2024Jurysix international experts, one of them lives with ME/CFS
  4. 10/20247 funded€695,445 in total
  5. 01/2025Projects start12 to 15 months each
