ME/CFS and post-COVID have much in common, which points to the immune system. But how antibodies in the blood relate to the bacteria in the gut is little understood.

The project measures antibodies in blood, stool and saliva of people with ME/CFS, with post-COVID and with neither, and compares them. Shared or different patterns could lead to new ways to diagnose and treat both.

Original title: “Deciphering systemic and mucosal antibody repertoires against the microbiota in ME/CFS and PCC”
