It is a
beginning.

Since 2024, €3.1 million has gone into ME/CFS research in Vienna. Projects have started, researchers have joined the field, and a biobank is being built.

The way it was done, many small projects first and then a few large ones, is now being used beyond Vienna.

What continues

  • 2026–2029The WE&ME Foundation funds research internationally with about €2 million: first 6 to 7 exploratory projects, then 1 to 2 consolidating projects.
  • from 2027Two WE&ME Awards a year for early-career researchers.
  • from 2027In Vienna, 1 to 2 consortia continue what worked. → 1 call
WWTF and the WE&ME Foundation are separate funders.
Most ME/CFS research in Austria is paid for with private money.→ €3.1 million
Sources: WWTF, CrunchME, networking event 06/2026, WE&ME Foundation · as of 10/2026
