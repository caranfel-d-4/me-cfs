reviewed together with patients

People with ME/CFS have been part of the review since the first call in 2024. In every jury meeting, one seat belongs to an expert by experience: a researcher who lives with ME/CFS.

In 2026, people with ME/CFS also reviewed the proposals for the Consolidation Call in writing, before the jury met.

Reviews in writing

Before a jury decides, outside experts read each proposal and write a review. In 2026, members of Science for ME were among them: an online forum where people with ME/CFS discuss research. They read the same proposals and answer the same questions as the scientists.

Jury and reviewers work unpaid.
