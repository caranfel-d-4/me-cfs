€3.1 million

Since 2024, about €3.1 million has gone into ME/CFS research in Vienna. Most of it is private money: from the WWTF, co-funded by the WE&ME Foundation, and from donations.

Almost two thirds of it, the Consolidation Call, are not awarded yet. The decision falls in October 2026.
