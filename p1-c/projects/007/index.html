Studies have found that in ME/CFS the mitochondria, the power plants of the muscle cells, work less well.

The project uses magnetic resonance spectroscopy, a form of MRI, to look at the metabolism of skeletal muscle and heart in people with ME/CFS and with long COVID, and how it relates to their symptoms. The findings are meant to inform future treatment.

Original title: “The role of the skeletal muscle and myocardial metabolism in ME/CFS: in vivo MRS study.”
