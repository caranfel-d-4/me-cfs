Many people with ME/CFS have trouble thinking and concentrating. The project asks whether their brain uses oxygen differently.

With modern MRI, it looks at the brain’s metabolism, how it clears waste and how blood flows through it, in people with ME/CFS and in healthy people, and compares this with their symptoms. The aim is markers for a more precise diagnosis and for new treatments.

Original title: “Investigating Cerebral Oxygen Metabolism Dysfunction as a pathomechanism of Myalgic Encephalomyelitis/Chronic Fatigue Syndrome”
