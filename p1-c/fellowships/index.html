6 fellowships

Six early-career researchers spend about six months in another lab: three go from Vienna to labs abroad, three come to Vienna. They learn new methods and bring them back, and they build contacts early in their careers.

The fellowships are open to researchers who have not worked on ME/CFS before. The first fellows started in May 2026.
