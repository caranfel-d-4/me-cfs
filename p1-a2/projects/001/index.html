SevereME/CFS

Project
ME-CFS24-001
Team
Kathryn Hoffmann · MedUni Wien
Funding
€99,752
Period
01/2025 to 03/2026

About one in four people with ME/CFS is severely or very severely ill. They carry a heavy burden of symptoms and complications and get too little medical care. Their families, who care for them, get too little support.

The project looks at their situation in Austria. In interviews and focus groups with patients, carers and health professionals, it asks what care they need, and gives recommendations for future research, especially on telemonitoring and care at home.

Original title: “Adult patients with severe and very severe ME/CFS in Austria. A multi-perspective study.”
