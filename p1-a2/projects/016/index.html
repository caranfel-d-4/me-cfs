Many proteins in the blood carry chains of sugar. When these are built wrongly, they can drive inflammation and autoimmunity.

The project uses mass spectrometry to test whether these sugar patterns show how severely someone is ill, especially in the crashes after exertion. Later, treatments could try to restore them.

Original title: “Exploring the Glycome: Identifying Glycosylation-Related Biomarkers for severity stratification in Chronic Fatigue Syndrome”
